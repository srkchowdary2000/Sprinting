# Gym Day Revision

Your original exercise list is useful, but too many heavy or eccentric exercises in one weekend session can create soreness and reduce sprint quality.

Van Hooren et al. 2024 suggests 3-4 exercises of 3-4 sets per session for sprinters. This version keeps 3-4 main lower-body lifts, rotates the rest across weeks, and moves the no-equipment exercises to the grass ground.

## Best Exercise Priorities

Tier 1:

- Jump squat.
- Squat.
- Romanian deadlift.
- Power clean or clean pull.

Tier 2:

- Hip thrust.
- Nordic curl. For injury prevention it is Tier 1, since it halves hamstring injury rates (van Dyk et al. 2019).
- Walking lunge.

Tier 3:

- Hip flexion with band or cable.
- Pull-ups or muscle-up practice.
- Dips or push-ups.

From your list, clean and jerk vs cleans + military press: the clean is the sprint-relevant part. The jerk and the press are upper-body fitness only.

## Recommended Session

Every week:

- Jump squat: 3-4 x 3-5, bodyweight to about 30% of squat 1RM.
- Squat: 3-4 x 3-5.
- Nordic curl: 2 x 3-6.
- Pull-ups: 3 x 4-8.
- Dips: 3 x 6-12.

Week A adds:

- Romanian deadlift: 3 x 5-6.

Week B adds:

- Power clean: 3-4 x 2-3.
- Hip thrust: 3 x 5-8.

Done at the ground instead (Wednesday bodyweight block):

- Walking lunges or split squats: 2 x 8 each leg.
- Band hip flexion: 2 x 10-15 each leg.
- Push-ups: 3 x 10-15.

## If Time Is Limited

Minimum effective gym day:

- Jump squat: 4 x 3.
- Squat: 4 x 3-5.
- Romanian deadlift: 3 x 5.
- Nordic curl: 2 x 3-5.
- Pull-ups and dips: 3 sets each.

## Loading Rules

- Move every rep fast on the concentric.
- Leave 1-3 reps in reserve.
- Do not grind heavy reps. End a set when reps slow noticeably. Stopping at about 20% velocity loss gave the same strength gains and bigger jump gains than 40% (Pareja-Blanco et al. 2017).
- Keep jump squats light. At 80% of 1RM, athletes got slower over 20m (McBride et al. 2002), and bodyweight gives peak jump-squat power (Cormie et al. 2007).
- Do not take Nordics to failure.
- If sprinting within 24-48h, reduce RDL, lunge, and Nordic volume.
